# Skylink Bingwa — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [set on publish]**

Skylink Bingwa ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android app for buying Safaricom
"Bingwa" data, SMS, minutes and special offers from a single seller — the My
Bingwa business. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and
does not do with your information.

Skylink Bingwa has no customer accounts, no passwords and no cloud sync. The details
you enter stay on your own phone. We designed the app to collect as little as
possible.

---

## 1. What data we collect

**Stored only on your device (not sent to us):**

- **Your name and primary Safaricom number.** You enter these once when you
  first open the app so it can greet you and pre-fill your number at checkout.
  They are saved locally on your phone. They are not used to create an account.
- **Recipient numbers you buy for.** When you buy a bundle for another number,
  that number is remembered locally so you can reuse it quickly.
- **Your activity.** Your purchase records (offer, recipient, amount, time and
  status), your favourites, and your app settings are stored locally on your
  phone.

**Shared only to complete a payment you start:**

- **Payment details for an online purchase.** When you tap Pay, the app sends
  the selected offer, the bundle recipient number and the M-Pesa payment number
  to the Skylink Bingwa payment server (`mybingwa.blazetechscope.com`) so it can start
  the M-Pesa STK Push prompt on your phone. The actual payment is processed by
  **Safaricom M-Pesa**. Your M-Pesa PIN is entered on the Safaricom prompt, never
  in this app, and it never reaches us.
- **An M-Pesa receipt you choose to enter** for an offline (Till/Paybill)
  payment, sent to the payment server only so a real payment can be matched to
  your purchase.

We keep the amount of personal data we handle to the minimum needed to sell you
a bundle.

---

## 2. How we use your data

- To let you browse offers, favourite them and buy again quickly.
- To pre-fill your number and show your purchase activity on this device.
- To start an M-Pesa payment and tell you its honest status (for example
  "Payment received", "Payment failed" or "Waiting to verify"). The app confirms
  **payment only** — it does not track or confirm bundle delivery.
- To show you optional, meaningful notifications if you turn them on.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not build a
profile of you across other apps or websites.

---

## 3. What we do NOT collect

- **No accounts, passwords, OTPs or cloud backups.** Nothing you enter is synced
  to a server to identify you.
- **No browsing or usage tracking.** The app cannot and does not see your web
  browsing, your other apps, your remaining mobile data, or your Safaricom
  airtime balance.
- **No location data.**
- **No contact list.** The app never reads your contacts. You type recipient
  numbers yourself.
- **No SMS access at all.** The app does not request permission to read your
  messages, on any build.

---

## 4. Permissions and why the app asks for them

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| **Internet** | To load offers and to start and check M-Pesa payments through the payment server. |
| **Network state** | To detect whether you are online or offline, so the app stays usable offline and can show M-Pesa Till/Paybill instructions instead. |
| **Notifications** (Android 13+) | Optional. Only requested after we explain it in the app. Used for payment updates and, if you allow them, offer reminders. You can decline and the app still works. |
| **Phone state** | Requested only when you tap "open M-Pesa" for an offline payment, and only to pick the SIM matching the number you told us is yours so the M-Pesa menu opens on the right line. It is best-effort; if you decline, the app falls back to your default SIM. The app does not silently read or upload your phone number. |

Skylink Bingwa does not request permission to read your SMS messages, on any build.

---

## 5. Data sharing

We share the minimum needed to process a payment you start:

- **Safaricom / M-Pesa** receive the information required to run the STK Push and
  process the transaction (this is how M-Pesa payments work).
- The **Skylink Bingwa payment server** receives the offer, recipient number, payer
  number and (for offline purchases) any receipt you enter, only to start and
  verify that payment.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers or any third party for
marketing. The app does not currently include third-party analytics or
advertising software. If crash-diagnostics or analytics are ever added, they
would use an **anonymous installation ID, never your phone number**, with
sensitive values masked; full phone numbers, M-Pesa receipts and PINs are never
logged.

Payment-related secrets (such as M-Pesa/Daraja credentials) live only on the
secure payment server. They are never stored inside the app.

---

## 6. Data retention and your control

- Your profile, favourites and activity live on your device for as long as the
  app is installed.
- You can wipe them at any time using **Settings → Clear local data** in the app,
  or from your phone's Settings → Apps → Skylink Bingwa → Storage → Clear data.
- **Uninstalling the app removes all of this local data.** Because there is no
  cloud account, it cannot be recovered afterwards, and reinstalling starts
  fresh.
- Records that reach the payment server or Safaricom as part of a real M-Pesa
  transaction are retained by those systems under their own policies and Kenyan
  financial record-keeping rules.

---

## 7. Children

Skylink Bingwa is intended for people old enough to hold an M-Pesa account and buy
mobile bundles. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children.
