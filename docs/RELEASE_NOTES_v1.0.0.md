# Skylink Bingwa v1.0.0 — Release notes

Welcome to Skylink Bingwa, the simplest way to buy Safaricom Bingwa bundles.

Skylink Bingwa lets you find and pay for **data, SMS, minutes and special offers** in
just a few taps — for your own line or for someone else's.

## What you can do

- **Browse every offer** by category — Data, SMS, Minutes and Special offers —
  with clear prices and validity.
- **Search, filter and sort** to find the right bundle fast, and **favourite**
  the ones you buy often for quick rebuy.
- **Buy for yourself or for another number.** Just enter the number that should
  receive the bundle.
- **Pay with M-Pesa.** When you're online, approve the M-Pesa prompt right on
  your phone. When you're offline, the app shows you the exact Till or Paybill
  steps so you can still pay.
- **Always know what's happening.** Clear, honest payment states — Payment
  received, Payment failed, Waiting to verify — with no guesswork. If you close
  the app mid-payment, it picks up where you left off.
- **Works without internet.** Offers, favourites and your activity stay available
  offline; the catalogue refreshes automatically when you reconnect.
- **Helpful, optional notifications.** Turn on updates for your payments and
  saved offers whenever you like — and turn them off just as easily.
- **Light and dark themes** that follow your phone's setting.

## Good to know

- No account, password or sign-up needed. Your name, number and activity stay on
  your phone.
- The app confirms your **payment**. Your bundle is delivered separately — after
  payment, just wait for it to arrive on the number you chose.

This is our first release. Thank you for using Skylink Bingwa.
